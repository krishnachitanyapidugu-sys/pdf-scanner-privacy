Privacy Policy

PDF Scanner · Last updated 2 October 2026

Your documents stay on your phone

PDF Scanner turns paper documents and photos into PDF files on your Android phone. In short: the app does not collect your data, and it has no way to send your documents over the internet.

What stays on your device

Pages you scan with the camera or import from your photos, the PDFs the app creates, folder names and your settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app processes them on the device, and other apps cannot read them.

No data collection, no internet access

The app does not ask for internet permission. It does not collect, upload, sell or share personal information, and it contains no advertising or analytics tools. We have no access to your documents.

Permissions

  • Camera is used only to scan documents. Images from the camera are processed on your phone.
  • Photos you import are chosen with the Android photo picker, so the app can only see the photos you select.

Sharing

A document leaves the app only when you choose Share. A copy of that PDF is then handed to the app you pick (for example email or a messaging app), and that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

Backup and deletion

Your documents are excluded from Android cloud backup. They can move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer. Deleting a document removes it from the app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
